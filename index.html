Our Story

A Legacy of Flavor. A Passion for Hospitality.

Zaika means “a sense of flavor”—a celebration of the rich aromas, spices, and traditions that define Indian cuisine.

At Zaika Indian Cuisine, we bring together authentic modern and traditional Indian dishes inspired by the many spices and flavors of India. Every dish is prepared fresh to order using premium ingredients, aromatic spices, and time-honored cooking techniques.

Our Executive Chef trained and worked at the world-renowned Taj Mahal Hotel in India, bringing years of culinary experience and a passion for authentic Indian cuisine to the Treasure Coast.

Our menu offers a unique approach to traditional, ethnic, vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dishes, with each plate individually prepared to order.
